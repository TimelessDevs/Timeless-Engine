Timeless Engine is a cross platform [Friday Night Funkin'](https://github.com/FunkinCrew/Funkin) Engine aimed at simplifying modding, optimizating and adding customization.
Previously known as a Glitch Engine.

> [!NOTE]
Please keep in mind that, despite these differences, we do not consider our engine to be any better or worse than the others.
